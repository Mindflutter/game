# S3 — One boss, one complete layer: implementation plan

**Built 2026-09-29.** The record is `changelog.md` and D62–D65; this plan is
history now.

> Written by Claude for Claude. Igor, 2026-09-29: "just write a plan for
> yourself and implement immediately, natively". Inline execution; no review
> gate; **no commit steps** — the commit waits for Igor's `/simplify`
> (`CLAUDE.md` → Skills and plugins). History once built: where it disagrees
> with `decisions.md`, the decision wins.

**Goal:** a layer that can be *finished*: two mini-bosses along the way and a
boss as its last batch, both lunging with a readable tell.

**Architecture:** content first — two new `ENEMY_TYPES` records carrying a
`lunge` field (the behaviour and its timings) and three new `BATCHES` entries.
One function, `lungeStep()`, runs the lunge in the existing enemy loop; the
chasers keep their line untouched. Render adds a swell and a lane during the
wind-up. The bot learns to start from a scenario, so the boss fight can be
measured alone (D63).

**Tech:** plain JS, Canvas 2D, Vitest + fast-check (dev only).

## The design (approved in chat, 2026-09-29)

Settled by Igor: D62 (the boss is the layer's last batch), D64 (the boss
lunges), D65 (a mini-boss is a smaller boss); D63 (the win rate is the boss
fight's) is Claude's call, delegated. Parts 1–3 below were presented and
approved ("approved", "looks right", "everything else looks ok"); the help-line
proposal was declined — Igor: "help screen doesnt need this trivial
explanation".

**1. The layer's shape.** D54's twelve batches stay as they are. A mini-boss
batch goes after batch 4 and another after batch 8, and the boss is last: 15
batches, 14 ranks. Each holds its lunger alone. The boss rises from the bottom,
centred (Igor: "The boss swims in from the bottom"); mini-bosses arrive from a
random edge like any batch. Mini-boss ~24 hp, boss ~60 hp — 10s and 24s of pure
shooting at the flat 2.5 hp/s. New scenario `mini-boss`; `last-batch` becomes
the boss.

**2. The lunge.** Chase (slower than the player) → wind-up (stands and swells,
a faint lane shows its path; the line is fixed when the wind-up starts, aimed
where the player was) → charge (along the line at ~3× the player's speed, for
~450px or to the view's edge) → rest (still, the opening) → chase. Boss wind-up
1.0s, mini-boss 1.5s; rest 1.2s. Contact costs a heart like any enemy. The dash
shoves and wounds it like anything else. No health bar.

**3. The ending, the measurement, the checks.** The boss's clear is the layer
end — no rank, no mods (D59) — and "LAYER CLEARED" becomes the real ending.
The bot fights the boss (`last-batch`) and the mini-boss (`mini-boss`) alone,
mortal, starting kit, five seeds; plus how far it gets from the top. The two
`logic.js` comments that promise S3 a layer-owned batch list are reworded
(approved), keeping the `ponytail:` marker.

**Claude's picks** (to be marked in code and docs): every number in 1–2 — hp,
radius, chase speed, the chase minimum before a wind-up (1.0s), wind-up, charge
speed 780px/s, reach 450px, rest, `value`, colours; placing the mini-bosses
after batches 4 and 8; the boss's spawn point (bottom centre, 40px + its
radius below the view); winding up only when fully inside the view.

## Global constraints

- `game/` gains no browser API; the port seam stays six names (`npm test`).
- Plain objects in flat arrays; a behaviour is a field plus a branch (D5, D26).
- Every tuning number lives in `game/content.js`.
- Squared distances in the per-frame loops; `Math.pow(x, dt)` for decay.
- Every behavioural change lands with a check that fails without it —
  verified by breaking the rule.
- Glossary words exactly: boss, mini-boss, batch, gate, layer end.

## Review focus

Failure modes the design implies that no task's test pins, most likely first:

1. **A tell nobody sees** — a lunger winding up while partly off-screen. Pinned:
   it winds up only fully inside the view, and Task 1's property test asserts
   the view holds it from the wind-up on.
2. **A charge that carries the fight off-screen.** Pinned by the same property.
3. **The dash shoving a lunger mid-wind-up** — the lane moves with it, the
   direction stays locked. Accepted; the property still requires the line not
   to turn.
4. **The boss rising under a player parked at the bottom edge** — its body
   hurts on contact like any arrival's; it cannot charge until fully in view.
   Accepted: the same rule as every other arrival.
5. **A spurious clear at tick one when the bot starts from a scenario** — the
   "begun but unrecorded" test assumes `batchIndex` starts at 0. Pinned by
   Task 4's bot test.
